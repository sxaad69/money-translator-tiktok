Privacy Policy — The Money Translator TikTok Integration

Last updated: October 2026

This integration connects the TikTok developer app "The Money Translator" to its owner's TikTok account to publish the owner's own videos. It is not offered to third parties and collects no data from anyone else.

Data we access

  • Basic account info (via TikTok Login Kit, scope user.info.basic): used only to authenticate the account owner during setup.
  • Video upload (scopes video.upload, video.publish): used only to upload the owner's own video files to the owner's TikTok account.
  • OAuth tokens: access and refresh tokens are stored securely on the owner's private server and used only to maintain the connection. They are never shared.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect, store, or process data about any TikTok user other than the account owner, and we do not sell or share any data with third parties. No analytics or tracking is embedded in this integration.

Data retention

OAuth tokens are kept only while the integration is in use and are revoked/deleted if the integration is disconnected.
